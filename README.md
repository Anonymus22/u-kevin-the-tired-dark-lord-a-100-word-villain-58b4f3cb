# The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue

A 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired.
The tiredness lives in the voice, the rhythm, the pauses and the small
deflections - never in a plain statement. He is still a villain: nine
kingdoms conquered before lunch, a general's betrayal at dawn, and tonight
an eclipse ritual to reclaim the crown his father stole from the sea-kings.

`main.ts` writes the monologue to `entry.txt` (exactly 100 words, plain ASCII).
